Job Title: AI/ML Engineer
Experience: 2 years

Job Description:
We are looking for an AI/ML Engineer with around 2 years of
professional experience to design, build and deploy machine
learning and Generative AI applications.

Responsibilities:
- Build, train and evaluate machine learning and deep learning models.
- Develop LLM-powered applications using LangChain.
- Build Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines with vector databases.
- Develop and deploy model-serving APIs using Python and FastAPI.
- Clean, process and analyze structured and unstructured data with SQL and Python.
- Containerize and deploy applications using Docker.
- Collaborate with engineering and product teams to ship AI features.

Requirements:
- 2 years of professional experience in AI/ML or a related role.
- Strong Python programming skills.
- Solid understanding of machine learning and deep learning concepts.
- Hands-on experience with LLMs, LangChain and RAG.
- Experience building REST APIs with FastAPI.
- Working knowledge of SQL.

Preferred:
- Experience with Docker.
- Experience with a cloud platform (AWS, GCP or Azure).
- Experience with vector databases (FAISS, Chroma, Pinecone or similar).
